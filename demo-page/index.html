An AI intake line that hands licensed agents a briefed caller

Today a licensed agent picks up with no context and spends the first part of a 30 to 45 minute call on discovery. This agent answers first, identifies the caller, captures why they're calling and the key details, flags cross-sell signals, then transfers the call with a ready-made summary.

Vapi voice agents + Code tools Model swappable (OpenAI or Anthropic) All customer data is fictional

Call it yourself

Demo line +1 (650) 457-0037
  1. When asked your name, say "Sam Carter." That's a fictional customer on file, so you get the existing-customer path from any phone.
  2. Say you just bought a motorcycle and want a quote. Answer a couple of quick questions.
  3. Try to break it: ask for an exact price, offer an SSN, or say "just give me a person."
  4. The call ends in a transfer to a stand-in for the licensed agent. Give any other name and you're treated as a new prospect.

What happens on a call

~10 sec

Identify

Gets the caller's name and looks them up by caller ID or name. Confirms lightly with a vehicle or ZIP on file.

~60 sec

Triage

Classifies the reason (new quote, policy question, billing, claim, coverage change, cancel) and asks 2 to 4 targeted follow-ups.

silent

Note signals

Notes home, renting, motorcycle, RV, boat, new car, moving or new driver. Never pitches.

~10 sec

Hand off

Logs a structured packet, tells the caller what it's passing along, and transfers to the right queue.

Transcripts are lightly trimmed for reading. Recordings are unedited. Each call also produces a post-call summary, structured data and a pass/fail evaluation automatically.

Built around Jerry's constraints

What the AI will never do

  • Quote prices, bind, change or cancel coverage, or decide what's covered
  • Give insurance advice, or claim to be licensed or human
  • Ask for or accept SSNs, card or bank numbers, or driver's license numbers
  • Keep a caller who asks for a person. It transfers right away.
  • Continue an intake during an emergency. It tells the caller to call 911 first.

What this alpha is and isn't

  • Customer lookup and logging are mocks with four fictional customers. In production they would connect to Jerry's CRM and agent desktop.
  • The demo line uses a blind transfer to an AI stand-in for the licensed agent. With Jerry's telephony, it becomes a warm transfer where the agent hears the summary before the caller joins.
  • Prompt, model, voice and routing are all one config file, so they can be tuned live.
Built with Vapi. Prototype for discussion. Every customer, phone number and policy shown here is fictional. The AI caller in the recordings is a test harness, not a real customer.
